# WobbleBook Pro Touch 🍐

**Live:** https://littlebigz.github.io/wobblebook/

A fictional 3D parody of the touchscreen-laptop trend. Tap the screen, and the lid wobbles.

> "If you give a laptop a touchscreen… you'll want to tap it. If you tap it… it'll want to wobble."

## What this is
- **WobbleBook Pro Touch** by **Pear** is a made-up product, and "Pulp OS" is a made-up interface. This is satire about vertical touchscreens on laptops. It is not a review of, and makes no claims about, any real product or company.
- The page plays a 10-second 3D film, then lets you tap the laptop yourself. The lid is a damped spring: every tap adds an impulse, and some touches register late or not at all, just for laughs.
- All code, 3D models, the UI and the sound are original. Everything is procedural: Three.js geometry, a canvas-drawn UI, a GLSL glitch shader and Web Audio synthesis. It uses no third-party logos, images or footage.
- It's a single self-contained `index.html` that works offline, with no tracking or analytics.
